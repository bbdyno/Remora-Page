pricing

Free to use. Pro if you want sync.

The free version has no time limit and no ads. Remora Pro adds iCloud sync across your Apple devices.

Three ways to get Pro

All three unlock the same thing. Planned pricing in US dollars, may vary by region.

Monthly

$2.49 / month

A subscription billed each month through your Apple account.

Yearly

$19.99 / year

A subscription billed once a year through your Apple account.

Lifetime

$39.99 one-time

A single purchase. Not a subscription, so nothing renews.

Prices shown are planned and may change before release. The price shown in the App Store at the time of purchase is the one that applies.

How billing works

  • Purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment card details.
  • Monthly and yearly plans are subscriptions that you manage in your Apple account settings. See how to cancel a subscription.
  • Remora runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac (iOS 18, iPadOS 18, macOS 15 or later). There is no version for other platforms.
