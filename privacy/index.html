Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Remora ("the app"), published by bbdyno ("we", "us"), handles your information. The short version: we do not collect it.

Effective date: October 12, 2026

  • Remora has no account and no sign-up.
  • We run no servers and receive no data from the app.
  • There are no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • Your data stays on your device, or in your own iCloud if you turn on sync.

1. Information we collect

None. Remora does not send us any information about you, your device, your servers or how you use the app. We operate no servers for the app, so there is nothing on our side for the app to send data to.

Remora does not include analytics, advertising or tracking code, and it does not include third-party SDKs that collect data.

If you turn on app lock, it uses your device's Face ID, Touch ID or passcode through the system. The app never receives your biometric data.

2. Information stored on your device

To do its job, Remora keeps the following on your device:

  • App data: your host list, saved command snippets, port-forwarding rules and the known host keys of servers you have connected to.
  • Credentials: passwords and SSH private keys. These are stored in the Apple Keychain on your device. They leave the device only if you turn on iCloud sync, as described below.

This information is not transmitted to us.

3. iCloud sync (optional, Pro)

iCloud sync is off unless you turn it on. If you do, your hosts, snippets, port-forward rules and known host keys sync between your Apple devices through your own private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit), and your passwords and private keys sync through iCloud Keychain, which Apple encrypts end to end.

That data is held in your iCloud account and handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We cannot access it.

4. Connections to your servers

SSH and SFTP connections go directly from your device to the servers you configure. They do not pass through any server of ours. The servers you connect to receive whatever any SSH client would send them, such as your network address and the username you sign in with, and they handle it under their own operators' policies.

5. Purchases

Remora Pro purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple may provide developers with aggregated sales and usage statistics that do not identify you, and, only if you have chosen to share them in your device settings, diagnostic and crash information.

6. When you contact us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in the message. We use it only to reply to you and to resolve your request.

7. Your control over your data

Because your data lives on your device and, if you enable sync, in your own iCloud account, you are in control of it. We hold no copy, so we have nothing to disclose, export or delete on your behalf. iCloud data can be managed in your Apple account's iCloud settings.

8. Children

Remora is a tool for people who administer servers and is not directed at children. As described above, the app does not collect personal information from anyone.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the updated version on this page and update the effective date above.

10. Contact
